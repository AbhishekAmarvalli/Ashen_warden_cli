PROLOGUE — THE TOMB OF COLD ASH

One distant bell, and then the ash is still.
You open your eyes beneath a cracked visor. Gray dust covers the insignia on
your chestplate; an amber glow pulses once behind your ribs, weak as a dying
coal. Your fingers close around the weathered hilt of a silver sword.

Around you: broken standards, ruined wagons, and the outlines of the dead in
the ash. This was a battlefield — and you were meant to be part of it.

A whisper slips through the dust, neither near nor far:
"When the final bell tolls, return to the throne."

You remember almost nothing: an oath, a burning hall, and the weight of chains.
The ember in your chest will not let you rest.

Goals:
  - Read the damaged journal beside you (interact journal)
  - Inspect and equip your blade (inventory -> inspect 1 -> equip 1)
  - Learn this place (look, go, search, help)
  - Speak with whoever still draws breath (talk keeper)
  - Cut down whatever blocks the way out (go north)
