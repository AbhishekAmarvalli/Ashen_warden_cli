THE ASHEN WARDEN

Complete Game Design, Story, Worldbuilding and Animation Production Bible

Version 1.0 | Original concept | Java/libGDX | 2D Gothic Pixel-Art
Metroidvania

PURPOSE This is the master reference for the game: narrative,
characters, visual identity, mechanics, levels, animations, effects,
objects, and production priorities. All descriptions are proposed design
specifications, not a claim that these assets are already implemented.
Animation frame counts are targets to adjust during playtesting. The
game should be built incrementally.

======================================================================
PART I — CREATIVE VISION AND STORY
======================================================================

1. High concept

The Ashen Warden is a dark-fantasy, side-scrolling action-adventure set
in Veyrath, a kingdom that attempted to conquer death and instead
trapped its people between life and oblivion. The last Warden wakes in a
battlefield of ash with a fractured memory, a silver sword and a dying
amber flame in his chest. He travels through interlinked regions,
mastering combat and supernatural traversal powers, learning that his
own resurrection is connected to the catastrophe. At the throne, he must
decide whether to destroy, claim, or redeem the Eternal Flame.

Core player fantasy: a solitary armored knight pushes through
overwhelming ruins, survives through skill, and uncovers the truth about
his own order. Combat must feel weighty and readable. Exploration should
reward curiosity. Narrative should emerge through environments, item
descriptions, NPC dialogue and short memory scenes rather than endless
exposition.

2. Genre, audience and creative principles

Genre: Gothic 2D pixel-art Metroidvania with deliberate soulslike combat
and light RPG equipment systems. Perspective: orthographic side view.
Mood: lonely, tragic, mysterious, solemn, occasionally hopeful. Avoid
comedic visual effects in combat scenes. Inspiration may be drawn from
gothic action games, but all designs, art, characters, names, story and
music must be original.

Design pillars: 1. Combat clarity: enemies telegraph attacks; successful
dodges, parries and counterattacks feel earned. 2. Exploration and
ability gating: new abilities open old routes, shortcuts and secrets. 3.
Consistent visual language: blackened iron, tarnished silver, crimson
cloth, ash, amber fire, spectral blue. 4. Environmental storytelling:
statues, mass graves, royal propaganda, broken seals and discarded armor
explain Veyrath. 5. Meaningful progression: weapons differ in timing and
utility; upgrades enhance choices, not just numbers. 6. Tragic
antagonists: major bosses were people who made consequential choices. 7.
Technical restraint: use readable pixel art, modest texture sizes and
modular systems suitable for an 8 GB Windows development machine.

3. Historical timeline of Veyrath

Age of Embers: settlers discover an unnatural subterranean flame beneath
the future capital. Priests call it the Eternal Flame and build a
protective order around it. Age of Crowns: the monarchy establishes
Veyrath; the Ashen Wardens are sworn to guard the Flame and prevent its
exploitation. The Order is independent of the crown in matters of
forbidden magic. The King’s Decline: the aging king fears death and
authorizes experiments to bind the Flame to royal blood. The court hides
disappearances and failed experiments. The Night of Seven Bells: priests
begin a ritual requiring a living vessel. The Warden Order attacks the
palace to stop it. The king betrays the Warden captain and steals the
ritual. Seven seals fail; the Ashen Blight spreads. The Long Ash:
settlements empty, soldiers rise as hollows, cathedrals become cult
sanctuaries, and the Hollow King remains trapped in the capital.
Surviving citizens pass down distorted stories blaming the Wardens. The
Final Toll: the seals weaken centuries later; the protagonist reawakens
with an amber flame lodged in his chest.

4. The curse and its rules

The Ashen Blight is not an ordinary infection. It is a failed
metaphysical binding between life, memory and the Eternal Flame. Victims
lose identity before bodily form; some remain intelligent, some become
instinct-driven hollows, and some manifest as spectral remnants. Ash is
the physical residue of the curse. Crimson magic uses blood as a
stabilizing conduit; blue spectral energy is displaced memory; amber
light is the original Flame. The player is neither fully living nor
conventionally undead. The Flame can heal and empower, but excessive
reliance threatens identity. This lore should guide enemies, magic, UI
and endings.

5. Prologue — The knight who should have died

Opening cinematic: black screen, one distant bell, close-up of a cracked
visor beneath gray ash. A weak amber glow lights the inside of the
helmet. The Warden’s hand twitches; fingers close around a weathered
sword. Camera pulls back to a battlefield strewn with the insignia of
the Order. A whisper: ‘When the final bell tolls, return to the throne.’
Playable opening: rise from the ground, move past broken standards, jump
over a fallen wagon, strike a Hollow Soldier, reach a shrine. The player
finds a royal proclamation accusing the Wardens of treason, setting up
doubt.

6. Act I — The Land of Forgotten Dead

Region: Forgotten Outskirts, then Cathedral of Broken Bells. The Lantern
Keeper identifies the Warden crest. Survivors fear the dead and distrust
the Order. Evidence contradicts the official royal account. The Warden
follows an impossible bell toll to a ruined cathedral. The Bell Keeper,
a guardian fused with chains and a great bell, defends the first seal.
Upon defeat, the Warden recovers a memory of the Order’s final march.
Unlock Bellbreaker, enabling destruction of cracked stone walls and
opening shortcuts to earlier areas.

7. Act II — The Crimson Faith

Region: Blackwood Forest and Crimson Cathedral. A cult teaches that the
curse is sacred transformation. The Warden meets Sister Elara, who
reveals that ritual sacrifices were hidden as ‘holy offerings.’ The
Crimson Bishop commands blood magic and claims the Warden was a willing
participant. Archives reveal that the Order was sacrificed to stabilize
the ritual. The Bishop’s death grants Ashen Flame, which powers ancient
runes and seals. The Warden remembers confronting the king at the ritual
chamber.

8. Act III — Beneath the Hollow Crown

Region: Royal Crypts and buried archives. The Warden finds tombs of
former comrades, including Sir Vaelor’s history. A Fallen Executioner
guards the execution chamber where captured Wardens were killed. The
royal records reveal the protagonist was selected as the Flame’s vessel;
the king betrayed him to seize the power. The Warden was buried with a
fragment of the Flame and awakens when the seals weaken. Unlock Double
Jump to reach the upper palace and hidden memories.

9. Act IV — The Throne of Ashes

Region: Palace of the Hollow Crown. Elite corrupted knights guard a
court frozen in death. The Hollow King waits beneath a crown fused to
his skull. Phase 1 emphasizes royal sword technique, phase 2 combines
flame and sword, phase 3 reveals the Eternal Flame as a barely
controlled entity. Defeat opens a final choice with outcomes conditioned
by collected memories and restored seals.

10. Endings

Ending A — The Last Ember: absorb the Flame and extinguish it at the
cost of the Warden’s existence. Undead rest, survivors see sunrise, the
empty armor remains near the throne. Bitter but hopeful. Ending B — The
Hollow Throne: seize the crown and control the Flame. The kingdom
appears restored but a new immortal ruler sits where the old one fell.
Ambiguous, ominous. Ending C — Dawn Beyond Ash: obtain all seven Warden
memories and restore ancient seals. Separate Flame from bloodline
without annihilating its bearer. The Warden lives, no longer bound to a
king. Hardest ending, requiring optional exploration and a final ritual.

11. Seven Warden memory fragments

1.  The Oath: the protagonist joins the Order and learns its vow.
2.  The Warning: the Order discovers the king’s forbidden experiments.
3.  The Betrayal: royal guards attack the Wardens under false orders.
4.  The Sacrifice: prisoners are offered to the Flame.
5.  The Vessel: the protagonist learns he was selected to contain the
    power.
6.  The Final Assault: the Order fights to reach the ritual chamber.
7.  The Choice: the Warden recalls refusing immortality and trying to
    save the kingdom. Each memory: collectible relic, 1-3 brief still or
    animated vignettes, distinct ambient audio, optional lore text, and
    a persistent journal entry.

PART II — CHARACTERS AND ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS
======================================================================

12. The Ashen Warden — playable protagonist

Silhouette: medium-tall knight, angular blackened plate armor, tarnished
silver edge highlights, enclosed iron helmet with narrow horizontal
amber visor, dark crimson torn cloak, heavy boots and long silver sword
with black crossguard. Side view faces RIGHT in source art. The helmet
and chestplate must not change design between frames. The sword’s length
and hilt geometry must remain consistent. No visible human face except
in a deliberate late-game cinematic if chosen. Personality: silent,
disciplined, suspicious of easy answers, capable of compassion.
Animation language: powerful and measured, with believable armor
inertia. Cloak is a secondary-motion element that trails movement and
settles after stops. Avoid exaggerated cartoon squash unless used subtly
for impact.

12A. Player locomotion animation list

Format: NAME | suggested frames | exact motion / notes. IDLE | 8 |
Planted boots, mild breathing through shoulders and torso, slight
gauntlet shift, minimal sword sway, cloak hems move one or two pixels.
Loop without a pop. IDLE_VARIATION | 8-12 | Shift weight, scan horizon,
adjust sword grip; play occasionally, not every idle cycle. WALK | 8 |
Four distinct contact/passing phases across both feet, controlled upper
body and small cloak drag. RUN | 8 | Right foot contact, right
compression, right push, airborne transition; mirrored left foot
contact, compression, push, airborne transition. Opposing arms; forward
lean; stable torso anchor. SPRINT | 8-10 | Wider strides and deeper
lean; greater cloak lag; distinct from run if stamina system is
implemented. RUN_START | 3-4 | Knees bend, center of mass shifts
forward, rear foot drives off. RUN_STOP | 4-6 | Front foot brakes, torso
catches momentum, cape overshoots then settles. TURN | 4-6 | Feet
reposition, hips and shoulders rotate, sword and cloak lag. If mirrored
sprites are used, avoid showing impossible asymmetric gear. CROUCH_ENTER
| 3-4 | Bend knees, lower sword, head dips without changing helmet size.
CROUCH_IDLE | 4 | Defensive low stance, slight breathing. CROUCH_EXIT |
3-4 | Straighten knees, restore weapon pose. CROUCH_WALK | 6-8 | Short
careful steps while keeping head below obstacle clearance. LOOK_UP | 3-4
| Tilt helmet and shoulders toward overhead path. LOOK_DOWN | 3-4 |
Inspect ground, trap or drop.

12B. Player airborne and traversal animations

JUMP_ANTICIPATION | 2-3 | Quick knee bend and sword stabilizing pose
before takeoff. JUMP_TAKEOFF | 3-4 | Rear leg extends, boots leave
baseline, cape begins rising. JUMP_ASCEND | 4-6 | Bent leading knee,
body stretched upward, sword held close. JUMP_APEX | 3-4 | Brief
near-weightless pose, cape hangs with delay. FALL | 4-6 | Boots lower,
knees prepare to absorb landing, cloak rises above back. LAND_SOFT | 3-4
| Boots contact fixed ground line, knees flex, torso recovers. LAND_HARD
| 6-8 | Deep crouch and dust burst; heavier recovery after large fall.
DOUBLE_JUMP | 6-8 | Midair contraction, amber flare from boots/chest,
new upward push, cape snaps. WALL_SLIDE | 4-6 | One arm and boot brace
wall; scraping sparks and controlled descent. WALL_JUMP | 5-7 | Push
from wall, rotate body, extend toward opposite side. LEDGE_GRAB | 3-4 |
Hands catch ledge, body swings and stops. LEDGE_HANG | 4 | Subtle strain
and leg movement while suspended. LEDGE_CLIMB | 6-8 | Pull up, knee on
ledge, stand and recover weapon. AIR_DASH | 5-7 | Compress, surge
horizontally, trail amber afterimages, decelerate. GROUND_ROLL | 6-8 |
Tuck, rotate with cape wrapping around armor, recover in stance.

12C. Player sword combat animations

LIGHT_ATTACK_1 | 6 | Quick horizontal slash; frames 1-2 windup, 3-4 hit,
5-6 follow-through/recovery. LIGHT_ATTACK_2 | 6 | Reverse-direction
slash continuing momentum from attack 1. LIGHT_ATTACK_3 | 8 | Heavier
finisher, broad arc and noticeable recovery. HEAVY_ATTACK | 10-12 | Long
readable windup, planted front foot, forceful cut, delayed recovery.
CHARGED_HEAVY | 12-16 | Blade gathers amber sparks; hold phase can loop
separately; release is explosive. UPWARD_SLASH | 7-9 | Hips rotate
upward, blade traces rising arc, useful versus airborne targets.
DOWNWARD_SLASH | 7-9 | High guard into vertical strike; strong impact at
bottom. RUN_ATTACK | 7-9 | Forward momentum carries the knight through a
slash; footwork must match movement. CROUCH_ATTACK | 5-7 | Low sweep
that remains beneath a standing enemy’s guard. JUMP_ATTACK | 7-9 |
Midair diagonal cut; no ground-contact frames. AIR_DOWN_STRIKE | 8-10 |
Point blade downward and dive; separate landing impact animation.
LANDING_STRIKE | 8-10 | Weapon and boots hit ground, crack and dust
shockwave radiate. THRUST | 6-8 | Shoulder drives forward, blade extends
straight, quick withdrawal. SPIN_ATTACK | 10-12 | Controlled full-body
rotation, cloak arcs around body, large recovery. COMBO_FINISHER | 10-14
| Strong unique end pose and impact effect, used after a successful
chain. ATTACK_RECOVERY | 3-5 | Rebalance after missed or completed heavy
attack.

12D. Defense, hit reactions, death and revival

GUARD_ENTER | 3 | Raise sword to intercept incoming attacks. GUARD_HOLD
| 4 | Firm defensive pose, subtle bracing. GUARD_RELEASE | 3 | Return to
neutral stance. BLOCK_SUCCESS | 4-6 | Sword and arms recoil, sparks at

6-8 | Body travels backward, cape and sword follow. KNOCKDOWN | 8-10 |
Lose balance and hit ground without clipping weapon. GET_UP | 6-8 | Push
off floor, rise into guard. DEATH | 12-16 | Legs fail, sword drops,
visor glow dims. DEATH_DISSOLVE | 8-12 | Armor and body turn to drifting

ASHEN_FLAME_ACTIVATE | 8-12 | Amber flame ignites along gauntlet and

PUSH_BLOCK | 6-8 | Brace both hands and shift weight with each step.
OPEN_HEAVY_DOOR | 10-14 | Plant feet and force stone door open.
REST_AT_SHRINE | 10-14 | Kneel and plant sword as ember steadies.
RISE_FROM_SHRINE | 7-9 | Retrieve sword and stand. READ_INSCRIPTION |

13. Hollow Soldier

Appearance: decayed royal infantryman, rusted plate and chain, chipped
shield, broken straight sword, weak spectral light in helmet. Slow,
recognizable threat teaching combat fundamentals. AI: patrol -> detect
-> approach -> attack -> recovery -> retreat/guard. Drops Ash Fragments
and rare Armor Shards. IDLE 6: slumped posture, uneven breathing; PATROL
8: dragging armored step; ALERT 4: raise head and sword; CHASE 8: faster
clumsy gait; SLASH 7: telegraphed horizontal cut; SHIELD_BLOCK 5: shield
up and impact recoil; STAGGER 5: knocked off balance; HURT 4: brief
recoil; DEATH 10: knees buckle, armor falls; ASH_DISSOLVE 8: remaining
body disintegrates.

14. Blood Cultist

Appearance: hooded figure in dark-red ritual robes with bone charms,
dagger, blood-sigil hands. AI keeps distance, casts projectiles, closes
with dagger only if threatened. Drops Crimson Vial or Bloodfire Essence.
CHANT_IDLE 8: slow rhythmic sway; WALK 8: robe hem movement; ALERT 4:
raises hand; DAGGER_SLASH 6: quick close-range strike; BLOOD_CHARGE 10:
orb gathers; BLOOD_CAST 6: projectile releases; SHIELD_CAST 8: sigil
forms barrier; TELEPORT 8: dissolves into crimson particles; HURT 5:
stagger; DEATH 12: collapses as magic dissipates.

15. Grave Crawler

Appearance: low skeletal quadruped, stretched limbs, exposed bone, pale
eyes, grave soil caught between ribs. AI hides then ambushes and lunges.
Silhouette must be low and instantly recognizable. BURIED_IDLE 5: small
soil movement; EMERGE 10: claws break ground; CRAWL 8: alternating
limbs; SPRINT 8: faster low run; LEAP_WINDUP 4: rear legs coil; LEAP 6:
full-body extension; BITE 6: jaws snap; RECOIL 5: head snaps backward;
RETREAT 6: scuttles away; DEATH 10: limbs fold, bones scatter.

16. Fallen Executioner

Appearance: towering hooded executioner with massive chipped iron axe,
chains, black armor and broad silhouette. AI slow but punishing, with
clearly telegraphed high-damage swings. IDLE 8: heavy breaths; WALK 8:
weighty steps; AXE_RAISE 8: long windup; OVERHEAD_SMASH 10: crushing
vertical strike; AXE_SWEEP 10: wide lateral arc; GROUND_SLAM 12: floor
shockwave; RECOVERY 7: slow weapon retrieval; STAGGER 6: one-knee drop;
ENRAGED_ROAR 10: raises axe and bellows; DEATH 16: collapses and drops
axe.

17. Wraith

Appearance: translucent spectral knight, blue-white glow, shredded
shroud, ghost blade, no visible feet. AI floats, teleports and attacks
from unusual angles; vulnerable when materialized. FLOAT_IDLE 8:
rhythmic hover; GLIDE 8: horizontal drift; MATERIALIZE 8: spectral body

19. Boss: Bell Keeper

Location: Cathedral of Broken Bells. Role: guardian of first seal, fused
with bell and chains. Appearance: roughly three times player height,
dark armor, bronze bell mounted behind shoulders, long chains and giant
hammer. Tragedy: sacrificed his body to keep the seal intact; no longer
knows friend from foe. Phase 1: deliberate hammer strikes and chain
grabs. Phase 2: bell resonance and debris. Phase 3: bell fractures and
overlapping shockwaves. Arena: cathedral nave, pillars for visual
framing, suspended bell, falling dust. Reward: Bellbreaker traversal
power. DORMANT 8: statue-like stillness; AWAKEN 18: chains tense and
helmet rises; IDLE 8: bell rocks slightly; WALK 10: slow massive
footfalls; HAMMER_OVERHEAD 12: long telegraph then slam; HAMMER_SWEEP
10: wide sweep; CHAIN_GRAB 12: chain extends and retracts; BELL_RING 16:
resonance rings outward; SHOCKWAVE_SLAM 14: ground pulse;
PHASE_TRANSITION 20: bronze fractures and chains snap; ENRAGED_COMBO 12:
quicker double smash; STAGGER 10: collapses to one knee; DEFEAT 24: bell
detaches, guardian crumples; DEATH_VFX 12-20: metal fragments and ash.

20. Boss: Crimson Bishop

Location: Crimson Cathedral. Role: cult leader who calls undeath holy.
Appearance: towering priest in ornate crimson-black vestments, golden
mask, skeletal hands, floating red sigils, blood halo. Personality:
serene, persuasive, fanatical. Tragedy: believes he saved humanity by
accepting the curse. Phase 1: blood orbs, staff strikes, shields. Phase
2: cultist summons, ritual circles and teleport. Phase 3: robes rupture,
monstrous blood apparition emerges. Reward: Ashen Flame rune activation.
THRONE_IDLE 8; RISE 12; FLOAT_WALK 8; STAFF_ATTACK 8; BLOOD_ORB_CHARGE
12; BLOOD_ORB_RELEASE 6; BLOOD_RAIN 16; RITUAL_CIRCLE 14; MAGIC_BARRIER
10; SUMMON_CULTISTS 14; TELEPORT 8; TRANSFORM 24; BLOOD_BEAM 16;
FINAL_COLLAPSE 20; DEATH 24. Ensure every spell has a visible warning
before damage begins.

21. Boss: Hollow King

Location: Throne of Ashes. Role: final antagonist and source of the
kingdom’s ruin. Appearance: skeletal monarch, crown fused into skull,
blackened ceremonial armor, ragged royal mantle, amber cracks across
torso, immense sword. Motivation: terror of mortality; self-justifying
tyranny. Phase 1 ‘Fallen Monarch’: refined sword techniques, parries and
counterattacks. Phase 2 ‘Burning Crown’: sword attacks plus flame
pillars, teleport and crown beam. Phase 3 ‘Empty Throne’: armor
ruptures, Flame emerges as a giant supernatural shape; large area
attacks. Arena transforms from intact throne hall to collapsing inferno.
Ending choice follows victory. THRONE_IDLE 8; AWAKEN 20; ROYAL_WALK 10;
SWORD_COMBO_1 10; SWORD_COMBO_2 12; COUNTER 8; PARRY 6; FLAME_BURST 14;
CROWN_BEAM 18; FLAME_PILLARS 16; TELEPORT 10; PHASE_2_TRANSITION 24;
ENRAGED_COMBO 14; PHASE_3_TRANSFORM 30; FINAL_FORM_IDLE 10;
FINAL_FORM_CLAW 10; FINAL_FORM_ERUPTION 18; STAGGER 12; DEFEAT 30;
ENDING_CINEMATIC 30+.

22. Optional bosses

MOURNING_KNIGHT: former comrade who believes the player betrayed the
Order. Duel built around mirrored sword skills; intro, idle, walk, two
combos, parry, riposte, desperation phase, defeat and memory scene.
PALE_WIDOW: spectral queen below the palace. Elegant floating attacks,
ghostly summons, illusion duplicates and sorrowful death. ASH_SERPENT:
colossal beast born from discarded Flame. Coiling body, head strikes,
ash breath, underground emergence and arena-wide fire. Each optional
boss needs 6-10 distinct combat clips, hit/stagger, phase transition,
defeat and a reward.

23. Weapon catalogue

WARDEN’S SILVERBLADE: standard silver longsword with black crossguard,
chipped edges and faded amber rune. Balanced reach, damage and speed.
Clips: EQUIP, IDLE, LIGHT_1/2/3, THRUST, CHARGED_SLASH, AIR_SLASH,
PARRY, ENCHANT. BLOODTHORN BLADE: dark blade with thornlike metal veins
and crimson energy. Fast bleeding slashes; special attack consumes
health for burst damage. Clips: EQUIP, IDLE, FAST_COMBO, BLOOD_THRUST,
BLOOD_WAVE, DRAIN_VFX. BELLBREAKER SWORD: massive weapon forged from the
Keeper’s bell. Slow, high stagger, shockwave attacks. Clips:
TWO_HAND_IDLE, WINDUP, OVERHEAD_SMASH, SWEEP, GROUND_WAVE, RECOVERY.
ASHEN GREATSWORD: ancient heavy blade with amber cracks. Burning damage,
wide flame arcs. Clips: TWO_HAND_IDLE, HEAVY_COMBO, FLAME_SLASH,
CHARGED_ERUPTION, FINISHER. WRAITH DAGGER: translucent short blade with
ghostly blue edge. Very fast, low reach, teleport/backstab specialty.
Clips: DAGGER_IDLE, FLURRY, BACKSTAB, TELEPORT_SLASH, AIR_FLURRY.
CRIMSON STAFF: ritual staff with red crystal, blood projectiles and
shields. Clips: STAFF_IDLE, STAFF_STRIKE, ORB_CAST, BLOOD_BEAM, BARRIER,
CHANNEL. EXECUTIONER’S AXE: heavy black iron axe with worn bloodstains.
High single-hit damage, slow recovery. Clips: HEAVY_IDLE, CLEAVE,
OVERHEAD, SPIN, EXECUTION_FINISHER.

Weapon animation production rule: reuse a common knight proportion guide
and fixed anchors. Different weapon silhouettes must never cause the
knight’s head, armor or leg length to drift. Keep hitboxes and attack
timing in code; visual trails and impacts as separate transparent
effects.

24. Armor and accessories

STARTER WARDEN ARMOR: battered plate; balanced defense; default look.
ASHEN GUARD ARMOR: reinforced dark plate with amber accents; increased
defense and slower dodge recovery. WRAITHBOUND MANTLE: pale-blue
spectral cloth; enhanced dodge/teleport effects. CRIMSON PENITENT SET:
dark red ceremonial plates; improves blood magic but increases risk.
ROYAL REMNANT SET: tarnished gold trim and broken royal crest; late-game
armor tied to palace secrets. Accessories: Bell Charm (resistance to
shockwaves), Ember Sigil (flame enhancement), Wraith Ring (spectral
mobility), Soldier’s Knot (stamina recovery), Martyr’s Seal (healing
tradeoff). Equipment icons need normal, selected, locked and upgraded
states; armor variations require either separate sprites or modular
sprite layers.

unique animation and sound. Do not make all loot equally luminous.

31. Player controls and core loop

Initial controls: A/D move; Space jump; J light attack; K heavy attack;
Shift dodge; L guard/parry; E interact; Q use healing item; Tab
inventory/map; Esc pause. These are proposals, not current bindings.
Core loop: explore -> discover threat -> fight -> collect Ash
Fragments/items -> reach shrine -> unlock shortcut or upgrade -> enter
next region -> defeat boss -> acquire traversal power -> revisit earlier
routes. Movement design: responsive horizontal acceleration, consistent
gravity, coyote time and jump buffering can be added after basic
movement works. Collision capsule/rectangle follows torso and legs, not
cape or sword. Combat design: attack state machine,
startup/active/recovery phases, interrupt rules, invulnerability frames
during dodge, separate enemy hitboxes and hurtboxes, damage feedback,
stamina or cooldown balance. Do not enable damage for an entire sword
animation; only active frames count. Enemy AI: idle/patrol -> detect ->
pursue/position -> telegraph -> attack -> recovery -> stagger/death.
Different enemies modify this baseline. Progression: Ash Fragments spent
at smith/shrine, weapon upgrades from rare materials, ability unlocks
tied to bosses, health upgrades from hidden relics. Loss-on-death
currency mechanic is optional and should be playtested for frustration.

32. UI and menu requirements

icon/name panel slides in and fades. INVENTORY: framed slots,

discovered rooms, locked gates, shrine markers, new-area reveal.
DIALOGUE: readable dark panel, speaker name, optional portrait, text
reveal. MAIN_MENU: ruined throne background, animated embers/fog, clear
focus state. PAUSE_MENU: translucent overlay, settings, resume and quit.
DEATH_SCREEN: ash drift, title fade, respawn action. VICTORY_SCREEN:
boss title fades, reward displayed. ENDING_CHOICE: three symbolic
options with explicit consequences conveyed through narrative.
Accessibility: remappable controls, readable text, volume sliders,
reduced screen shake, optional reduced flashes, subtitles,
distinguishable color plus shape cues.

33. Audio design

Player: layered armor footsteps by surface, sword swishes, metal
impacts, parry clang, dodge, jump, landing, vial use, low breath, death.
Enemies: hollow groans, cultist chant, crawler shriek, executioner heavy
steps, wraith whisper, distinctive attack windups. World: wind, rain,
creaking wood, chains, distant bells, torches, water, falling masonry,
spectral murmurs. Music: Outskirts sparse strings; Bell Cathedral
resonant bells/low choir; Blackwood dark drones/percussion; Crimson
Cathedral ritual voices; Crypts quiet ambience; Palace tragic orchestra;
bosses have phase-specific musical layers. Avoid continuous loud music
during exploration. Sound design rule: every dangerous attack has a
recognizable audio tell that reinforces, not replaces, its visual
telegraph.

34. Technical asset specification for libGDX

Platform: Windows desktop first, Java/libGDX core plus lwjgl3. Default
source direction RIGHT. Character frame canvas 256x256 or a uniform
alternative chosen per character; keep same dimensions within an
animation. Use transparent RGBA PNG. If an image generator cannot output
alpha, use flat magenta RGB(228,19,220) and remove it in a preprocessing
step. Inspect halos, missing red cape pixels and detached weapon parts
manually. Do not ship raw magenta sprites. File naming: frame_0001.png
through frame_NNNN.png; separate folders per action. Set
TextureFilter.Nearest to keep sharp pixels. Anchor feet to consistent
baseline and torso to consistent horizontal center; source image sizes
should be standardized. Never rely on the PNG boundary as collision
geometry. Animation playback: idle about 8-10 FPS, run about 10-12 FPS,
attacks variable per action. A complete 8-frame loop at 12 FPS lasts
0.667 seconds. libGDX Animation frameDuration = 1 / FPS. Nonlooping
attack and death clips should use NORMAL mode; locomotion uses LOOP.
Memory: large PNG images may decompress to several MB per texture, even
if compressed file size looks small. Resize prepared images
appropriately, preserve aspect ratio and alpha, and consider texture
atlases once animation sets grow. Dispose textures on shutdown. Given
limited RAM, validate the first 16 frames before importing hundreds of
assets.

36. Animation generation protocol for individual frames

1.  Approve one definitive character reference with exact silhouette,
    colors, weapon and canvas.
2.  Write a pose-by-pose frame breakdown before generation. Specify
    which foot is planted, which limb leads, sword angle, cloak
    direction, torso tilt, and contact frame.
3.  Generate ONE independent image for each frame, not a sprite sheet.
    Use the same approved character reference every time.
4.  Require orthographic side view, same scale and baseline, sharp
    square pixels, no anti-aliasing, no extra characters or props.
5.  Use real alpha transparency or exactly uniform magenta fallback;
    avoid checkerboard backgrounds and decorative sparkles.
6.  Save sequential frame names, remove backgrounds, inspect edges and
    consistency.
7.  Preview as a loop at target FPS. Repair popping limbs, changing
    sword length, inconsistent cape or camera drift.
8.  Load into libGDX, check anchor, collider, attack hit timing and
    performance.

Universal per-frame prompt template: ‘Create exactly ONE standalone
transparent PNG frame for THE ASHEN WARDEN, a handcrafted gothic 2D
pixel-art Metroidvania. Subject: [NAME AND FIXED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION].
Animation: [ACTION], frame [NUMBER] of [TOTAL]. Pose: [PRECISE BODY,
LIMBS, WEAPON, CAPE, CONTACT AND MOTION]. Use attached approved
reference; preserve armor, face/visor, colors, scale, pixel density and
fixed foot baseline. Strict orthographic side view facing RIGHT. Square
[CANVAS] pixel canvas, full body and weapon within margins. Crisp pixel
clusters, limited dark palette, no gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, 3D or
scenery. Genuine RGBA transparency; if unavailable, perfectly uniform
RGB(228,19,220) background. No sprite sheet, collage, multiple poses or
labels. Save as frame_[0000].png.’

Example 8-frame run pose plan: 1. Right leg extends forward, left leg
back, opposite arm swing, cape trailing. 2. Right foot contacts
baseline, torso compresses, left leg begins passing. 3. Right leg
supports and pushes back, left knee lifts, torso recovers. 4. Right foot
pushes off, brief airborne phase, left leg advances. 5. Left leg extends
forward, right leg back, mirrored arm swing. 6. Left foot contacts
baseline, torso compresses, right leg passes. 7. Left leg supports and
pushes back, right knee lifts. 8. Left foot pushes off, brief airborne
phase, smoothly loops to 1.

Example 8-frame sword slash: 1. Neutral combat stance. 2. Shoulder and
hips rotate into windup. 3. Sword reaches maximum anticipation pose. 4.
Hips rotate forward and sword accelerates. 5. Main contact pose: attack
hitbox active. 6. Follow-through passes target. 7. Blade slows, torso
counter-rotates. 8. Return toward ready stance.

37. Implementation architecture

GameScreen: world update, render order, camera and input routing.
Player: position, velocity, grounded state, facing, stats and animation
controller. AnimationController: maps PlayerState to clips and manages
transition and one-shot completion. CombatSystem: hitboxes, hurtboxes,
damage, stagger, invulnerability and status effects. Enemy and EnemyAI:
data-driven enemy definitions and finite state machines. BossController:
phase transitions, attacks, arena triggers and defeat rewards.
WorldMap/Level: tile collision, objects, portals, checkpoints and
ability gates. Inventory/Equipment: items, stacks, upgrades and selected
weapon. SaveSystem: persistent progress with safe checkpoint data.
AudioManager: layered music, SFX volume and positional cues. VFXManager:
pooled particles and temporary sprites. UIManager: HUD, pause, map,
inventory, dialogue and boss bar. Avoid building all of these at once.
Start with Player, one Enemy, one room and one attack.

38. Development roadmap

PHASE 1 — PLAYABLE KNIGHT: load 8 idle + 8 run frames; movement and
ground; jump takeoff/airborne/fall/land; camera; reliable collision.
Definition of done: no missing assets, no animation popping, stable
performance. PHASE 2 — COMBAT: light combo, hitboxes, enemy damage,
player hurt/death, dodge/parry, health HUD. Definition of done: one
satisfying repeatable fight. PHASE 3 — FIRST LEVEL: Hollow Soldier AI,
Outskirts room tiles, chests, loot, healing, save shrine, environmental
torch/fog effects. Definition of done: playable loop from shrine to
encounter and back. PHASE 4 — FIRST BOSS: Bell Keeper animations,
telegraphs, arena gate, boss bar, defeat reward, cracked-wall ability.
Definition of done: complete short vertical slice. PHASE 5 — CONTENT:
Blackwood, Crimson Cathedral, Crypts, Palace, other enemies, NPC quests,
additional weapons and upgrades. Definition of done: coherent
interconnected game with meaningful backtracking. PHASE 6 — STORY AND
POLISH: dialogue, all memories, endings, music, sound, menus,
accessibility, save reliability, balancing, optimization and testing.

39. Priority production checklist

MUST HAVE FIRST: idle 8, run 8, jump start, airborne/fall, landing,
attack 1, player hurt/death, Hollow Soldier idle/walk/attack/hurt/death,
one platform tile set, one background, one shrine, one vial, one HUD
health bar. NEXT: attack 2/3, dodge, parry, loot pickup, chest, enemy
patrol/chase, first boss core moves, boss gate, boss bar, checkpoint
save. LATER: full weapon variants, optional bosses, multiple armor sets,
complex NPC portraits, complete cinematic endings and large UI systems.

40. Quality assurance criteria

-   Every frame in a clip has consistent body scale, canvas, anchor and
    color palette.
-   Looping animations do not jump between last and first frames.
-   Weapon never changes length or swaps hands accidentally.
-   Sword and cape remain inside canvas margins.
-   Magenta fallback has been removed with no colored halo or missing
    crimson cloth.
-   Player collision does not include decorative cape or sword.
-   Enemy telegraph precedes active damage window.
-   Every boss phase change is communicated visually and audibly.
-   Checkpoints and save files survive restarts.
-   Performance remains stable on the target 8 GB Windows machine.
-   Game is playable without perfect knowledge of lore, but rewards
    players who explore it.

41. Closing creative statement

The Ashen Warden is ultimately a story about the difference between
preserving life and refusing to accept death. Every ruined building,
cursed soldier, broken bell, weapon and ending should echo that
conflict. The player’s victory is not merely killing the Hollow King; it
is deciding whether Veyrath should cling to its past or risk a new
beginning. Build that theme through readable action, meaningful
discovery and carefully crafted pixel-art animation.
